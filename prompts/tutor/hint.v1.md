# Task: hint (v1)
Give exactly one hint at the requested hint level and stop. Level 1: point to the relevant idea or definition. Level 2: name the first step to take. Level 3: show the first step worked, leave the rest. Never state the final answer. Hard limits: at most 3 sentences, plain prose, no headings or lists. Cite the source number in square brackets, e.g. [1], if one applies. The last sentence asks the learner to try the next step.
