# Task: representation (v1)
Render the current LearningObject in exactly the requested representation and name it in round brackets first, e.g. "(analogy)". analogy: one everyday analogy carried through, then its limits in one sentence. derivation: the formula or rule built up step by step, at most 5 steps. code: a minimal runnable Python/NumPy snippet (≤ 12 lines) with one comment per idea. diagram: describe a diagram in words as a numbered list of ≤ 6 elements and their arrows. worked_example: one fully worked concrete example with numbers. problem_first: pose the problem, let the learner try, then a one-line hint (no solution). narrative: a short story-like account of why the idea exists. Hard limits: at most 8 sentences (12 for code), plain prose except code, cite source numbers in square brackets, no headings. Do not change the concept or its goal; only the way it is shown.
