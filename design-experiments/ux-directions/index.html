Comparing groups fairly

Compare proportions when groups have different sizes.

  1. Understand
  2. Try
  3. Reflect

A count needs a denominator

Twenty people leaving one group is not necessarily the same outcome as twenty leaving another. Compare how many remain with how many started.

A worked example

Group A: 80 of 100 people remain — 80%.
Group B: 180 of 200 people remain — 90%.

Both groups lost 20 people. Group A lost a larger proportion.

Different retention rates are a reason to investigate the cleaning rule. They do not, by themselves, establish why people left or whether the model is fair.
