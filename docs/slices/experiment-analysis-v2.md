# Slice: experiment-analysis-v2 (P2, 2026-09-20)

**Story.** The n-of-1 dashboard must not overstate what a few sessions can show. v1 (Stage 4) treated every review as an independent observation and could print a zero-width interval for 3/3 successes. v2 makes the *assigned unit* the observation, uses intervals that stay honest on tiny or all-success samples, shows both unit and event counts, withholds directions without enough independent units, and says which outcomes a unit type cannot measure (review finding 5).
**Estimands.** For each metric and arm: the mean over assigned units of the unit's own mean (`delayed_recall`: share of delayed reviews rated Good/Easy; `error_rate`: share of wrong attempts; `latency_ms`: mean answer latency; `completion`: share of blocks not left early — session units only; `voluntary_continuation`: 1 if the session's *active* minutes (sum of `block_ended.actual_min`) exceed the plan — session units only, reported without a direction; `transfer`: mean transfer score). Ten reviews of one node are one observation with a within-unit mean; unequal observations per unit are handled by weighting units equally (the justified choice for an n-of-1 design where the unit, not the event, was randomised).
**Intervals.** Binary metrics: unit proportions → Wilson score interval per arm → Newcombe hybrid interval for the difference (never zero-width; all-success on 1 vs 1 unit spans the whole range). Continuous metrics: Welch t on unit means (Welch–Satterthwaite df, small-sample t table); no interval when there is no spread. `method` is reported per metric (`newcombe_wilson_units`, `welch_t_units`, `difference_only`, `insufficient`, `unavailable`).
**Direction.** "Hypothesis supported so far" only for the primary metric when both arms have ≥ `MIN_UNITS_FOR_SUPPORT` (10) independent units *and* the interval excludes 0; otherwise the wording is "No clear difference yet" or "Difference favours … — fewer than 10 independent units per arm: a reading aid, not evidence". `voluntary_continuation` is never read directionally.
**Window.** Learning events count while the experiment runs; delayed reviews and evidence for already-assigned units count for `OUTCOME_WINDOW_DAYS` (30) after Stop — the delay *is* the outcome — and nothing after that. Units with no events are counted in `units_without_data` and contribute nothing. A concurrently running experiment of the other unit type is reported as a confounding caveat.
**Arm fidelity.** Every `explained` turn under an experiment (node arm, else the session's arm) records `arm_intended` (the arm config), `questioning_style` (as requested) and — only when the stream completed — `arm_delivered`: for a **Socratic** arm the last sentence asks a question (trailing `[n]` citations ignored) and the turn has ≤ 6 sentences (2–3 concrete options after the question are allowed by the guardrails); for a **representation** arm the detected representation equals the requested one, and a mastery-gate refusal is recorded as `arm_not_applied`; an **explicit** arm is delivered unless refused — an explicit turn may legitimately end with a check question (guardrail 1), so no "asked instead of explained" test is applied to it. A unit with ≥ 2 verdicts that delivered the arm in fewer than half of them is **excluded** from the comparison and counted in `low_fidelity_units` (a single failed turn is reported as a caveat, never an exclusion); a cancelled stream gives no verdict. The model/route is on the same event. A failed Socratic response therefore cannot become evidence for a delivered Socratic treatment. Excluded units whose turns were refused by the mastery gate raise a separate "mastery confound" caveat.
**Attribution.** Events count from the unit's *assignment* time (a review of a node learned before the arm applied is not the arm's outcome). Transfer evidence is attributed through `evidenced.result.skill_id`. Session-unit experiments cannot attribute `delayed_recall`/`transfer` (the review happens in a later session) and say so; the short-vs-long template's hypothesis was rewritten accordingly. A finished or running experiment of the other unit type whose run overlapped is listed as a confounder.
**Versioning.** `Results.analysis_version = "v2"`; `measured` events carry `n_units`, `n_events` and `context.analysis_version`; historical `measured` rows (v1) are untouched and can be told apart by the missing version. `experiment_observation.n` holds event counts for v1 rows and unit counts for v2 rows — the table is not comparable across versions (the `measured` event is the audit record).
**Events.** `explained.context.{node_id, arm_intended, arm_delivered, arm_not_applied, questioning_style}`; `measured.result.{n_units, n_events}`, `measured.context.analysis_version` (EVENT-SCHEMA updated). No new verbs.
**UI.** `/experiments` shows the primary metric expanded and the others collapsed ("Other metrics"), units *and* events per arm, fidelity, one muted line for metrics the unit type cannot measure, the outcome window, caveats (incl. the repeated-looking warning) and the analysis version; `voluntary_continuation` is labelled "Ran past the plan … no direction is better"; nothing is coloured as a winner.
**Real-use protocol (owner).** 1) Create "Socratic vs explicit" from the template, Start. 2) Learn normally for two weeks; do not change `session.socratic` by hand on assigned nodes (the arm overrides it and the turn meta says so). 3) Read Results weekly; until ≥ 10 units per arm the page will say "reading aid". 4) Stop; keep reviewing for 30 days — delayed recall is what the hypothesis is about. 5) If `low_fidelity_units` is high, the local model is not delivering Socratic turns (known for llama3.1:8b): pull `gemma3-12b` or route `socratic` hosted before drawing conclusions. This slice verifies the *measurement*; a real two-week learning outcome stays pending.
