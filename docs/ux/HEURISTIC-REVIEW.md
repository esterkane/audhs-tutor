## Assessment method

Severity follows the owner's scale: **S0** cosmetic; **S1** minor friction; **S2** meaningful usability problem; **S3** serious problem; **S4** blocks task completion. Severity describes consequence, not implementation effort or an automated tool's label. Confidence and scope remain explicit. A server outage itself is not a UI defect; disguising it as empty content or unending loading is. No additional S4 interface defect is established by the current evidence.

Principles use [Nielsen's heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/): N1 system status, N2 familiar language, N3 control, N4 consistency, N5 prevention, N6 recognition, N7 efficiency, N8 focus, N9 recovery, N10 help. Progressive disclosure means keeping optional detail available without competing with the immediate task; it must not hide pause, recovery or essential context.

Accessibility mappings use the WCAG principles of perceivable, operable, understandable and robust. The [reflow guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html), [information and relationships guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html), and [name/role/value guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/name-role-value.html) frame follow-up verification. A 390px overflow observation does not establish a complete 320px reflow assessment; two-dimensional tables have exceptions. Missing h1 alone is not a conformance failure. An axe diagnostic is evidence to investigate, not a substitute for assistive-technology testing.

## Closed finding and unresolved hypotheses

**AU-01:** the shipped pause/end clarification remains verified for its bounded contract (navigation preserving server checkpoint, with late callbacks guarded). It does not suspend elapsed time or create multi-topic session history. Change topic still ends the current session and now discloses that consequence. Do not count the old hidden-end behavior as an unfixed S3.

**Adaptation transparency:** source offers reasons and accept/trial/reject/undo, but no actual adaptation proposal was triggered in the audit. Raw keys and title-only detail remain a hypothesis to reproduce, not a ranked confirmed defect. **Re-entry after a week**, physical headphone audibility, VoiceOver, comprehensive overlays/zoom, and owner comprehension are open gates. Simulated persona reviews cannot establish these human outcomes.
