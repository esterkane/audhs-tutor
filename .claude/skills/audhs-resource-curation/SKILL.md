Read docs/VISUAL-LEARNING-QUEUE.md and current source ingestion, settings, budgets and trust interfaces. Begin with curated local metadata and reviewed public terms. Offline cards do not make remote pages or players offline. Do not enable network providers, autoplay or automatic indexing from the kit's recommendations.

Build outbound queries from curated public tags or learner-reviewed text. A concept title may itself reveal private material. Test that notes, answers and corpus passages do not leave silently. Validate schemes, hosts, redirects, response limits and local-network exclusions on server fetches. Preserve license/attribution and original provenance; discovery does not authorize copying every asset.

Show at most a few relevant candidates with why/source/type and explicit open/load controls. Treat embedding permission as uncertain even after header checks; retain a link fallback. Official player parameters do not eliminate all recommendations, tracking or external navigation. Never promise a distraction-free third-party player; keep a clear return action. Verify provider behavior and licenses against primary sources when implementing.

Candidates remain drafts until accepted, separately from source truth or mastery. Use synthetic provider fixtures, quota/cache/timeouts, disabled-network and privacy tests. Preserve local-first routing; optional paid extraction needs existing budget/authorization checks. This skill does not govern unrelated owner-authorized acquisition or authorize new account access.
